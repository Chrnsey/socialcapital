How it works

Ways to get involved

None of it needs a budget to get started. Most companies begin with four or five staff who are willing to take part.

List staff as mentors

Employees who opt in are listed under their industry, and schools can ask them questions or invite them in for a visit. They only commit to what they agree to, and their contact details stay private.

Cover a talk

Pay a mentor's fee for their time and travel, usually €50 to €150, so a school can bring someone in without having to find the money itself.

Sponsor the platform

Annual sponsorship of €2,000 to €10,000 pays for the coordination that keeps the directory free for the schools that need it. In return you're credited as a partner.

Worth a conversation?

Let us know roughly what you have in mind, even if it's just a few willing staff to start with. We'll work out what fits from there.
