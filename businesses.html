What this looks like

Three ways to get involved

None of it needs a budget line to begin. The usual first step is four or five staff who are willing.

List staff as mentors

Employees who opt in appear under their industry, available for questions or a school visit. No obligation past what each of them agrees to, and their contact details stay private.

Cover a talk

The honorarium for a mentor's time and travel, typically €50 to €150, so a school can bring somebody in without finding the money themselves.

Fund the thing itself

Annual sponsorship of €2,000 to €10,000 pays for the coordination that keeps the directory free to the schools that need it. You are credited as a partner. That is the whole arrangement.

Want to talk it through?

Tell us roughly what you have in mind — a handful of mentors, a sponsored talk, or ongoing support — and we'll work out what fits.
