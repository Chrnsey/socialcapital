HEAR

A way into college on reduced points if your circumstances qualify.

HEAR offers college places on reduced points and extra support once you're in, to school leavers from disadvantaged backgrounds. It's ran by universities through the CAO.

It's assessed on your household, so you need to apply and show your circumstances.

What you actually get

A place on lower points than the published requirement, and support once you're there, such as study help, and in some cases money. It's the same course, exams degree as everyone else.

Check the deadline early

HEAR goes in with your CAO application, but its closing date is well before the main CAO one. Supporting documents are due even earlier. You are much better off looking for the deadlines at the start of 6th year rather than after the mocks

Paperwork

Proof of income and evidence of your circumstances. Start early and ask your guidance counsellor to help

Applying costs you nothing

If you're not eligible, your normal CAO application is used

What people get wrong

“I don't qualify”

It covers far more than you think, so it is better to apply and see what happens rather than not doing anything.

“People will know I got in on reduced points.”

It isn't on your degree and nobody in your lectures is told.
