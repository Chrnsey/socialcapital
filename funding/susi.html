SUSI

Covers fees and sometimes even maintenance grants

SUSI is the national student grant. Depending on your household income and circumstances, it can cover your student contribution and also pay you a maintenance grant to live on.

Applying is free, with the threshold higher than most people expect, so it's always better to try apply

Two separate things

One part covers your fees and the other pays you money to live on. They're assessed separately, so you might get one and not the other, and the maintenance grant is higher if college is far from home.

Apply early

You don't need an offer to apply. If you wait until August, you might be waiting until September for the money.

College-backed emergency fund

Most colleges run a Student Assistance Fund for students hit by costs they didn't see coming. It's discretionary and confidential.

What people get wrong

“My parents earn too much.”

The limits are higher than people think, and they change every year. Check the current ones on susi.ie.

“It only covers fees.”

There's also a maintenance grant. Paid directly to you, it can help fund your cost of living whilst studying.
