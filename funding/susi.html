Paying for it · The grant

SUSI

Covers fees for a lot of students and pays a maintenance grant to some. The most common mistake is assuming you won't qualify and never checking.

SUSI is the national student grant. Depending on your household income and circumstances, it can cover your student contribution and also pay you a maintenance grant to live on.

The income limits are higher than most people guess, and applying is free. Plenty of students end up paying for something they would have got, just because they assumed they were over the limit and never checked.

Two separate things

One part covers your fees and the other pays you money to live on. They're assessed separately, so you might get one and not the other, and the maintenance grant is higher if college is far from home.

Apply before you know where you're going

You don't need an offer to apply. If you wait until August, you could still be waiting for the money in September.

There's other money too

Most colleges run a Student Assistance Fund for students hit by costs they didn't see coming. It's discretionary and confidential, and far fewer people use it than could. Ask the access office about it in your first week, before you need it.

What people get wrong

“My parents earn too much.”

Maybe, but the limits surprise people and they change every year. Check the current ones on susi.ie.

“It only covers fees.”

There's also a maintenance grant, and it's paid directly to you.
