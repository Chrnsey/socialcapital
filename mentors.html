What it actually involves

As much or as little as you want

There's no script and no quota. Most mentors do far less than they expect to, and it's still enough.

Available for contact

Your profile goes up under your industry. Schools or students can reach out through the platform — you're never handing out your personal number or email.

A talk, if you want to

Some mentors give a 20-minute talk to a class once a year. Entirely optional, and we handle the logistics if you say yes.

Junior or senior, both matter

If you're a few years into your career, students often relate to you more than to someone senior. If you're 15+ years in, you bring the wider view. Neither is "better."

You set the pace

Answer a couple of questions a term, or get more involved later. Nobody's tracking a quota, and you can step back any time.
