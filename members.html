For students and past pupils. Over 18s only.

Contact mentors directly

Once you've left school, a network starts to really count. You might be looking for an internship, or just someone to read your CV and tell you honestly whether a job is worth going for.

Once you're signed in you can see the contact details mentors have chosen to share and get in touch with them yourself. It's just between you and the mentor, nobody else is copied in.

How to join

You can join if you're 18 or over and you go to, or went to, a school that's registered with us. Your school approves you, not us.

Still in school

Ask your guidance counsellor. Once you've turned 18 they can send you a link to sign up.

Left school

Get in touch with your old school and ask them to send you a link. It doesn't matter what year you left.

Your school is not registered

Email us and let us know which school you went to, and we'll reach out to them. It carries a lot of weight with a school when one of their own past pupils is asking.

Sign up

Use the link your school sent you and this will fill itself in. Make sure you use the same email address the link was sent to.

Use an email you'll still have in three years, not your school one, as those get switched off.

You must be 18 or over. We store your email, your school code and your date of birth, nothing else. See our privacy notice.
