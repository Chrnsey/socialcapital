For students and past pupils

A support network that's actually here to help

When you first leave school you'll start to notice where a network really starts to count, whether that's an internship at a big firm, or just being called for a nixer in your neighbourhood. Every person has a network which impacts them, and their work in a certain way.

Once you're signed in you can see the directory in full across a range of industries; ask questions about the job or the work involved in getting it. The volunteers are here to help, not to tell you what to do.

How to join

The network is limited to adults who are students or alumni approved by the website. If your school isn't registered yet feel free to reach out to them and put us in touch!

Students

Ask your guidance counsellor for a link to join. Over 18s are verified by school staff before being given their passkey

Alumni

Were you a student with one of our partner schools? Reach out to them to claim your passkey and we can get you set up on our platform.

School hasn't joined yet?

Email us and let us know which school you went to, and we'll reach out to them.
