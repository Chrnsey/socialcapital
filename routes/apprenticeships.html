Apprenticeships

Learn on the job, with a big salary by the time your friends have just finished college

An apprenticeship is a job with training included. You are an employee from day one and you split your time between working and a classroom. By the end of it you have practical experience and a qualification

Apprenticeships stopped being only trades some time ago. There are apprenticeships in software, insurance, accountancy, engineering and logistics. Some even come with a degree.

The hardest part is not the exams

It is finding an employer. There's no structured system the same way there is with the CAO. You have to get a company to say yes. Knowing somebody helps, which is the entire reason this website exists.

What people get wrong

“Trades are only practical fields”

Software, insurance, finance, accountancy, engineering, logistics. Read the list before you decide what you are.

“You'll earn less in the end.”

The power of earning money quickly... If you can get a mortgage sooner then you're already better off than those college grads paying 1k a month for a shoebox studio
