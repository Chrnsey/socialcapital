Too many choices · Usually cheaper than staying home

Studying in Europe

English-taught degrees across the continent at EU fee rates. A fraction of what the same thing costs in the UK, and sometimes less than Dublin.

Irish citizens, as EU students, get to pay the local rate anywhere in the union. In several countries that figure is small. Hundreds of degrees are taught entirely in English.

The Netherlands is the best known route. A large number of English language bachelor's degrees, a friendly application system, and student cities with a big international network. Germany and the Nordics teach in English too, at low or no tuition.

Entry works differently

Many European universities don't run a points race. Some admit anyone who meets a minimum standard, some use a lottery, some choose for themselves. A course that is unreachable on points here may not be unreachable there.

Housing is the actual problem

Not fees. Beds. Student accommodation in Dutch cities is genuinely scarce and has to be sorted early.

Check what Irish support follows you

Some Irish student supports apply to study elsewhere in the EU and some do not. Ask SUSI directly.

What you get that isn't on the certificate

Living somewhere else at nineteen changes what you think is available to you. If you have not travelled much, that is the larger half of the value.

What people get wrong

“I'd need the language.”

Hundreds of degrees are taught in English, particularly in the Netherlands and the Nordics.

“Abroad means expensive.”

EU tuition is often below the Irish student contribution.
