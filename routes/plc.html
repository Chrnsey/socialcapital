PLC and further education

A year or two, and you apply straight to the college.

A Post Leaving Certificate course is a full-time, practical course run by an Education and Training Board or a further education college. You apply directly. There is no points requirement.

The backdoor

Upon completion, many PLCs have an arrangement to do an additional year at a university to earn a degree. For some courses it is a more reliable way into the same degree than competing on points. Ask the college which courses their students move into after completing their PLC.

The deadlines aren't as set

No central system means no set closing date. Plenty of colleges take applications deep into the summer, including after results come out. It gives you plenty of opportunity to consider your options
