A directory for DEIS schools across Ireland

Give your students a network worth having.

Social Capital is a directory of professionals who've agreed to talk to your students — about their career, their path, and what the job's really like. Register your school and your guidance counsellor gets full access, free.

Register your school See how it works

There are more than 900 DEIS schools in Ireland, serving roughly 160,000 students. Access to the directory is free for every one of them.

How it works

What a school actually gets

A guidance counsellor registers the school, gets a login, and from there can browse the directory or ask us to arrange a talk. That's the whole process.

A directory of real people

Professionals across every industry — from people a few years out of school to senior leaders — searchable by sector and experience level.

Talks at your school

Request a speaker for a whole year group, or a smaller session for students with a particular interest. We handle the matching and the logistics.

Two tiers of mentor

Senior mentors bring experience and perspective. Junior mentors, only a few years out of school themselves, bring relatability. Both matter, differently.

Contact stays on the platform

Schools log in and arrange things through us. Students are never given a mentor's personal phone number or email directly.

Sectors students don't always get to see

Not just the obvious routes into work, but the fuller range of where a career can actually take you — including some that don't need a college degree to start.

Technology
Software engineering, product, UX design, data. Ireland hosts European HQs for Google, Meta, and Stripe, plus hundreds of Irish-founded companies.
Healthcare
Medicine, nursing, physiotherapy, pharmacy, occupational therapy — routes range from direct entry to postgraduate conversion.
Law
Solicitor, barrister, legal executive, or in-house counsel. Firms range from international practices to local general practice.
Finance
Banking, accountancy, insurance, fintech. Entry through ACA/ACCA/CIMA qualifications or direct graduate programmes.
Trades
Electricians, plumbers, carpenters, construction. Typically a SOLAS apprenticeship — paid, with no college fees.
Media & communications
Journalism, broadcasting, PR, marketing, content. A strong Irish tradition alongside a growing digital sector.
Education, engineering, public sector
Plus more inside — full profiles and requests are visible to registered schools only.
