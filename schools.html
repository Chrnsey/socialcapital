There are more than 900 DEIS schools in Ireland and roughly 160,000 students in them. This platform brings a network to those students

As part of the scale up of SocialCapital.ie we are offering free access to the platform for the first 5 schools to register. Those five stay free permanently. After that there will be an annual fee of €200 — and it stays free for any school where the cost is genuinely the obstacle. You only have to tell us.

How it works

What does the school get?

When a school registers with Social Capital it gains access to the directory, as well as the potential to request seminars and guidance talks. Your guidance counsellor can also give individual students and past pupils their own access, one at a time, so long as they are 18 or over.

Names, not job descriptions

Professionals across every sector, from people three years out of school to people running the place. The network is split by industry and experience level

Talks for groups - big or small

A whole year group, or six students with a specific interest, we're here to make career advice easy to access with real life impacts

Access for past pupils

Anyone over 18 can be given their own account, whether they are still in sixth year or left years ago. You send them a link, they sign up with a personal email, and you can take that access back at any time without asking us.

In the directory

Sectors in the directory

As well as the usual routes, the directory covers jobs that need no degree and pay from the first year.

Technology
Software, product, design and data. There's far more of this work in Ireland than you'd expect, and a lot of it is in Irish companies.
Healthcare
Medicine, nursing, physiotherapy, pharmacy and occupational therapy. Some people go straight from the Leaving Cert, others take a conversion course ten years later.
Law
Solicitor, barrister, legal executive or in-house. Firms range from large Dublin practices to one-solicitor offices, and the work is very different in each.
Finance
Banking, accountancy, insurance and fintech. Several of the qualifications can be done while you're working and getting paid.
Trades
Electricians, plumbers, carpenters and construction. A SOLAS apprenticeship pays from year one with no fees.
Media & communications
Journalism, broadcasting, PR, marketing and content. Journalism and broadcasting are long-established, while the rest moves fast.
Education, engineering, public sector
And more once you're registered. Full profiles are only for registered schools.

Full profiles are only visible to registered DEIS schools. That's part of the promise we make to our mentors.
