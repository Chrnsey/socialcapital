There are more than 900 DEIS schools in Ireland and roughly 160,000 students in them. This platform brings a network to those students

As part of the scale up of SocialCapital.ie we are offering free access to the platform for the first 5 schools to register. Those five stay free permanently. After that there will be an annual fee of €200 — and it stays free for any school where the cost is genuinely the obstacle. You only have to tell us.

How it works

What does the school get?

When a school registers with Social Capital it gains access to the directory, as well as the potential to request seminars and guidance talks. The schools can distribute network log in details to students and school alumni which allows interested individuals to browse the directory.

Names, not job descriptions

Professionals across every sector, from people three years out of school to people running the place. Searchable by industry and by how long they have been at it.

Somebody standing in front of a class

A whole year group, or six students with a specific interest. Email us and we find the person and organise it. You do not fill in a form.

Junior and senior, and the junior ones matter more than you'd think

Someone fifteen years in brings the wider view. Someone three years in is close enough that a student believes them. The second is harder to find and more useful than it sounds.

Nobody's phone number changes hands

Mentors' personal contact details are not visible to you or to your students. They sit in a separate place only we can open, and we make the introductions. That is on purpose.

After they leave, it keeps going

Once a former student is over 18 they can get their own account and contact mentors directly. That is when an internship is actually at stake, and it is the part nobody else does.

What's on it

The sectors nobody brings up

Not only the routes that get mentioned in January, but the rest of them, including several that need no degree and pay from the first year.

Technology
Software, product, design, data. An unusual amount of it for a country this size, and a good deal of it in Irish companies nobody has heard of.
Healthcare
Medicine, nursing, physiotherapy, pharmacy, occupational therapy. Routes run from straight after the Leaving Certificate to a conversion course a decade later.
Law
Solicitor, barrister, legal executive, in-house. From large Dublin practices to one-solicitor offices, and the work is nothing alike.
Finance
Banking, accountancy, insurance, fintech. Several of the qualifications can be earned while employed and paid, which students are rarely told.
Trades
Electricians, plumbers, carpenters, construction. A SOLAS apprenticeship pays from year one with no fees. The most under-explained option in the country.
Media & communications
Journalism, broadcasting, PR, marketing, content. An old tradition in the first two and a fast-moving industry in the rest.
Education, engineering, public sector
And more once you are in. Full profiles are for registered schools only.

Full profiles are visible to registered DEIS schools only. Not to us being precious about it — it is how we keep our word to the mentors.
